A first map of stochastic processes

Working note · Edit and expand as your understanding develops

A stochastic process is a collection of random variables indexed by time or another parameter. It gives a language for describing systems whose evolution is uncertain or noisy.

Questions to ask about a process

  • What are the possible states of the system?
  • How is uncertainty represented at one time?
  • How does the state distribution evolve?
  • Does the future depend on the full history, or only on the current state?

Markov property

A process is Markov when its conditional future, given the present and past, depends only on the present state. This is an assumption about the model, not a universal property of random systems.

Why it connects disciplines

Stochastic processes appear in statistical mechanics, noisy neuronal dynamics, probabilistic machine learning, and many models of interacting systems. The mathematical framework is shared, while the interpretation of states and transitions changes by application.
