Leaky integrate-and-fire neuron

Between spikes, the membrane potential follows a leaky equation. When it reaches threshold, the model records a spike and resets the voltage.

Model idea

A common simplified equation is \\(\\tau_m \\frac{dV}{dt}=-(V-E_L)+RI\\). Here the voltage relaxes toward a current-dependent steady level. Threshold crossing produces a spike event, after which voltage is reset.
